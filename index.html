Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Can I preview the converted result before downloading?

Yes. The workspace shows a live preview for supported conversions and provides a download button for the generated file.

Can I select multiple images at once?

Yes. JPG to PDF accepts 10, 20 or more image files and combines them into a single multi-page PDF in your chosen order with reordering support.

How does History work?

Your completed actions are stored securely in your browser's local storage so you can easily review past conversions.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

The supported browser-side conversions are performed locally on your device for maximum privacy and speed.
